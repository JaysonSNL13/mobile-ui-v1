Note for the dev team. Please do NOT change how the app scans or its safeguards — the scan flow and safeguards are the ones Yash already approved. Only the UI / format and the SCM Control Panel are changed here.

How to use

          
A quick recap before you start.

          
1Tap Begin Purge.

          
2Choose your purge list, or pick Free Purge to scan anything.

          
3Tap the camera and start scanning — follow the list, or for a free scan just scan whatever you want to purge.

          
4If some items won't be purged, exclude them with a reason and purge the rest.

          
5If something can't be purged, a pop-up explains why. Flag it for Ops review; once Ops approves, you can purge it next time.

          
6Tap Submit when done — it auto-creates the transfer in Brightpearl and sends the confirmation email.
